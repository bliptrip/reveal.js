## The "cycle" slide (A3) — agreed framing

Two cycles hide in the word "cycle": the *recurrent breeding cycle* (parent → parent) genuinely shortens with genomic prediction (choose parents / cull seedlings before they bear); the *cultivar release pipeline* (the program's >14 years) mostly keeps its calendar years — what changes is time-to-decision. Diagram levers, each placed where it acts: (1) markers before planting — identity, paternity, SC status, known QTL, predicted values → fewer, better-chosen trees enter the field; (2) measurement during single-tree progeny evaluation (≥ 4 harvests per FY2025) → more accuracy per harvest; early culling only if juvenile–mature r_g is high (CATIE splits ages 2–3 vs 4–7; rarely reported in cacao — a number to estimate, not assume); (3) repeatability in replicated clonal trials — the Rondônia 0.85-at-2-harvests / 0.93-at-5 numbers are clone-mean accuracies and belong here; a more repeatable phenotype shifts that curve left, G×year (year-pair r_g 0.41–0.94) does not move, and multi-site trials stay as G×E / release evidence; (4) a green loop from the phenotyped, genotyped trials back to year 0 = genomic prediction applied to the next cross. Phase lengths marked illustrative; invite Royaert to correct. The figure is a standalone editable file, `static/images/cycle/cycle_14yr.svg` (PNG at 3× beside it), loaded on the slide as an image; the slide text was cut to a title, one caption line and a citation, with the spoken walk-through in the notes (2026-09-21).

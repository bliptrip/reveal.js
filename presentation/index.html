Three terms, in plain words

  • QTL — a stretch of chromosome where the DNA differs between plants and the trait differs with it. Its marker can be read on a seedling, years before the tree is scored.

  • AUDPC — area under the disease progress curve: a whole epidemic, or a whole leaf-disc time series, as one number.

Backup · “How do you handle an allotetraploid?”

Four rules for markers in arabica

1 · Allo, not auto

Disomic: treat each locus as diploid within its subgenome. No dosage classes, no double reduction — the hard parts are homoeologs and introgressions

2 · Map to both

Align to a subgenome-resolved reference with C and E present, so reads choose their true home; a “het” in a pure-line parent is a homoeolog artifact until shown otherwise

3 · Filter with parents

Keep markers polymorphic between the parents and single-copy in their subgenome; KASP primers must be homoeolog-specific

4 · Expect blocks

Polymorphism clusters in Timor Hybrid segments with less recombination: big gaps, coarse intervals, linkage drag — background selection recovers the Typica / Mokka genome

Cranberry is diploid. The discipline transfers — containers, validation, parentage checks; the subgenome filtering I would build with the genomics group, on their assembly.

Evaluating a trait: three questions, then an economic one

Repeatable?

Same material, different day, different scorer — characterise the instrument before the germplasm

coffee: leaf-disc runs vary with inoculum load, spore viability and temperature — check cultivars in every run

Heritable?

Genetic share of what remains after run, rack and spatial effects, on the unit I select on

coffee: F2 plants and F3 families; the three-class score has no h² to report

Holds up?

Genetic correlation across the environments I deploy in — lab to farm, elevation to elevation

coffee: four mega-environments for rust in the WCR global trial; Kona’s 200–900 m slope

ΔG = i · ρ · σA / L — a trait I can measure perfectly that doesn’t change a selection decision isn’t worth measuring

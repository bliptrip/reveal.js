Three terms, in plain words

  • QTL — a stretch of chromosome where the DNA differs between plants and the trait differs with it. Its marker can be read on a seedling, years before the hill is harvested.

  • Heritability (h²) — the share of the differences between plants that is genetic: how much of what you see a parent can pass on.

  • Genomic prediction — a breeding value estimated from DNA across many small QTL. It can rank a male on cones he will never make.

Physiology

  • Stomata close early, at mild soil drying

  • Bine growth stops below about −0.8 MPa; xylem P50 ≈ −1.6 MPa
